About

A passion for discovery and for people.

Dr. Adebayo Kingsley Omoniyi is a lecturer, researcher, author, youth coach, pastor and youth conference speaker, driven by a passion for scientific discovery, education, leadership, youth development, faith and societal transformation.

Beyond academia, he is committed to raising purposeful young people through teaching, mentoring, coaching, conference speaking and pastoral ministry, helping individuals grow in knowledge, character, leadership capacity and a strong sense of purpose.
